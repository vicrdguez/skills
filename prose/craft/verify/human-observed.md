# Human observed

A person looks at the system and judges what they see: a screenshot, a recording, a walk through the product.

## Evidence

A person's judgement covers what no assertion captures: layout, wording, feel. It is evidence only for the person who made it, recorded with what they looked at and when.

## Catalog

A `human` row per review gate, with what to look at and how to record the judgement. A human row cannot prove a behavior rule; it backs the Manual Verification items a person closes. A verifier prepares what the person looks at, such as the capture or the running build, and leaves the judgement to them.

## Traps

- A capture from an earlier build shows that build; capture from the head under review.
- A gate that nobody owns stays open; name who closes it.
